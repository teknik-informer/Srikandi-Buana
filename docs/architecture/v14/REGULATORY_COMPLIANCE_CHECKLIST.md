# SRIKANDI BUANA — Indonesian Regulatory and Data Governance Checklist

**Purpose:** implementation checklist, not legal advice or a declaration of compliance.  
**Rule:** verify current official text, scope and applicability before enabling a production workflow.

## 1. Organization and legal identity
- [ ] Confirm current legal form and registration evidence.
- [ ] Store organization name, address, authorized representatives and document validity with source evidence.
- [ ] Do not label KWT as a cooperative, KUB, company or registered legal entity until the relevant evidence exists.
- [ ] Separate draft organizational policies from approved AD/ART, SK and meeting decisions.

## 2. Personal data protection
Reference: UU 27/2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi.
- [ ] Inventory personal data and purpose per processing activity.
- [ ] Define controller/processor responsibilities and vendor contracts where applicable.
- [ ] Record appropriate lawful basis/consent where required; consent is not the only possible lawful basis.
- [ ] Provide notices, access/correction mechanisms and complaint contact.
- [ ] Apply data minimization, role-based access, retention and secure disposal.
- [ ] Assess sensitive data and high-risk processing, including precise farm/household location and beneficiary data.
- [ ] Define incident response, breach assessment and notification process.
- [ ] Review international transfers and external partner access.

## 3. Electronic systems and PSE
Reference starting points: PP 71/2019 and Permenkominfo 5/2020 as amended.
- [ ] Determine whether the operator/system falls within private-scope PSE registration requirements.
- [ ] Confirm current registration, content, security, access and recordkeeping obligations from official sources.
- [ ] Maintain system/contact and incident records.
- [ ] Review third-party cloud, email, analytics and storage providers.

## 4. Employment
- [ ] Determine whether a person is a member, volunteer, contractor or employee based on actual facts and legal relationship.
- [ ] Enable payroll/leave/employment workflows only when relevant.
- [ ] Verify current employment rules, contracts, wage, social security and working-time obligations for the actual relationship.
- [ ] Do not classify all KWT members as employees.

## 5. Accounting and tax
- [ ] Determine the reporting entity and applicable SAK framework with a qualified accounting professional.
- [ ] Assess whether SAK EMKM or another SAK framework applies; do not infer applicability from organization name alone.
- [ ] Approve chart of accounts, accounting policies, fund restrictions and inventory valuation.
- [ ] Confirm tax registration and obligations with current DJP guidance.
- [ ] Version tax rules and effective dates; do not rewrite historical posted transactions.
- [ ] Do not claim statutory financial statements are correct until opening balances, cashbook and ledger are reconciled.

## 6. Business licensing and products
- [ ] Determine whether the organization carries out business activities requiring NIB or other licensing.
- [ ] Check current risk-based licensing rules, including PP 28/2025, and applicable KBLI/risk level.
- [ ] Verify product-specific requirements (food safety, labeling, distribution, certification) before public sale.
- [ ] Keep permit number, scope, issuer, issue/expiry date and source document.

## 7. Agricultural and international claims
- [ ] Confirm applicable EUDR scope, product/commodity coverage, operator/trader role and implementation dates from current EU official sources.
- [ ] Capture origin, plot, production, harvest, batch and chain-of-custody evidence with quality/verification state.
- [ ] Do not claim certification, deforestation-free status or regulatory compliance solely because data is recorded.
- [ ] Track external standards and audit evidence separately from internal self-declarations.

## 8. Regulatory configuration governance
Each rule/configuration must store:
- Jurisdiction and authority.
- Official source URL/document identifier.
- Effective date and review date.
- Applicable entity/activity and scope.
- Responsible reviewer/approver.
- Configuration version and change audit.
- Historical transactions remain linked to the rules applicable at the time.

## 9. Official-source review log
No legal conclusion is marked verified in this document. Before production release, add the official source, exact provision, review date, scope analysis and responsible reviewer for each applicable requirement.
