# SRIKANDI BUANA v13 — ToT and Pre-Agreement Template Pack

## Current status supplied by the group

The group's current engagement with prospective partners is at **Training of Trainers (ToT) / early engagement stage**. It has not reached a signed programme agreement or contract. The group does not currently have digital copies of the relevant programme documents.

For this reason:
- Do not create an active funded programme from a ToT invitation, meeting or training.
- Do not mark the group as a contracted implementing partner, certified group or beneficiary-provider for a specific scheme without the corresponding written evidence.
- Do not fill target participant counts, budgets, locations, dates, indicators or eligibility rules with estimates presented as facts.
- Record missing documents as **not yet received**, not as a failed or non-compliant requirement.
- Prepare adaptable templates now; configure exact requirements only after the partner supplies the latest official forms, agreement, guidance and reporting rules.

This status is an operational note supplied by the group, not a partner-issued status confirmation.

## 1. Opportunity lifecycle

Use one consistent lifecycle:

1. Reference identified
2. Initial contact
3. Training of Trainers (ToT)
4. Documents requested / pending
5. Written offer received
6. Proposal preparation
7. Proposal submitted
8. Negotiation / clarification
9. Agreement signed
10. Preparation for authorised activation
11. Active programme
12. Completed / closed
13. Paused / declined

The system must not permit a transition directly from ToT to Active. Before Active, the programme setup must be reviewed and the agreement or mandate verified. A signed agreement is still not enough by itself: programme budget, eligibility, data protection, safeguards, reporting and named delivery team must also be configured and approved.

## 3. ToT capture checklist

A ToT record should answer the following:

### Before or during training
- Which organisation hosted the training and which unit/team delivered it?
- What is the exact training title, location and actual date?
- Who facilitated it, and what role did each trainer have?
- Which SRIKANDI BUANA representatives attended?
- Which women-farmer group, unit or community did each participant represent?
- What learning objectives and topics were presented?
- Which materials, slide decks, manuals or official forms were actually provided?
- Was attendance full, partial, excused or absent?
- Was permission for photos/publication asked separately?

### After training
- What did participants learn and what remains unclear?
- Was an assessment actually performed? If yes, record its method and result. Do not invent scores.
- Was a certificate of attendance or trainer competency actually issued? Record its exact title, issuer, date and evidence only when seen. A ToT attendance record does not itself prove an accredited trainer qualification.
- What follow-up actions did the group agree to?
- Which actions have an owner and agreed due date?
- Which official documents are still awaited?
- What must be clarified with the partner before the group makes commitments?

## 4. Partner document intake workflow

When a document arrives, create a register row with:
- Partner and document title.
- Document type (invitation, training material, questionnaire, member form, programme guide, proposal, MoU/PKS, signed agreement, budget, indicator framework, reporting template, standard/annex).
- Source person/channel and received date.
- Version/document date and language.
- Private file reference and hash once the secure document vault is available.
- Verification state: not checked, source checked, current version checked, superseded, correction requested.
- Owner and next action.

The original document must be retained; do not replace it with a retyped summary. If a template is changed by a partner, preserve the prior version and link the replacement.

## 5. Pre-agreement readiness checklist

These are questions for internal preparation, not assumed requirements of every partner:

- Is the legal/organisational identity required by the partner clearly stated?
- Is the partner's official unit and authorised counterpart confirmed?
- Is the invitation/offer and programme scope written?
- Which locations, commodities, activities and groups are in scope?
- Who qualifies as a participant/beneficiary, and who decides eligibility?
- Are the programme period, workplan and reporting dates known?
- Is a budget or cost-reimbursement arrangement written and approved?
- What costs are allowed, which documents prove them, and what procurement thresholds apply?
- Which member information must be collected, for what purpose, and which fields may be shared?
- Who is responsible for safeguarding, grievance intake and incident referral?
- Are photo, location, household and partner-sharing permissions distinct?
- What indicators, units, baseline and target have actually been agreed?
- Which party verifies attendance, service delivery, quality and outcomes?
- What records and reports must be submitted, in what language and format?
- What are the termination, repayment, asset ownership, intellectual property and data-retention provisions?
- Who within the group has authority to approve and sign the agreement?

No missing answer should be converted into a failed compliance result while the partner has not yet issued the applicable requirement. Label it **Awaiting partner clarification**.

## 6. Coming-soon feature catalogue

Future modules are presented as capability roadmap items only. They must display “Segera hadir” with explanatory text, no active submission button, no fake record count and no empty working route. They become interactive only after:
- the data model and access policy are implemented;
- the required forms and source documents are verified;
- APIs, database migrations and audit trails are complete;
- role and negative permission tests pass;
- mobile/accessibility and import/export flows are tested; and
- the module has passed QA on an isolated, sanitised database.

The v13 future-module catalogue intentionally returns a null route and interactive=false for every future module. A later UI may display these cards without accidentally creating clickable dead links.

## 7. Template governance

Each template is assigned an ID and version. When a partner's official form arrives:
1. Save the original in the restricted document room.
2. Identify its version, issue date, scope and mandatory fields.
3. Compare the partner form with the existing template.
4. Create a new version or a partner-specific variant; never overwrite the generic template.
5. Mark personal/sensitive fields and their purpose, notice, retention and sharing permissions.
6. Test field validation, CSV/XLSX import/export, mobile entry and role permissions.
7. Obtain internal approval before using it for actual enrolment or reporting.

Template definitions are not legal advice and do not substitute for partner instructions, official government formats or certification-body requirements.

## 8. Practical first use

The first operational records should be:
- a partner engagement log for each actual ToT/discussion;
- one ToT session record for each training event;
- the actual attendance roster;
- a post-ToT action plan agreed by the group; and
- a document register listing all promised/requested/received files.

These records create a credible institutional history without pretending the group has already received a grant, signed a contract or achieved certification.
